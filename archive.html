2026

Annual General Body Meeting — MDB Summit 2026

The 2026 Annual General Body Meeting of Muchilottu Devasam Board was held at Kannur Town Hall. Key agenda items included new kaavu registrations, welfare fund review, and election of new executive committee members.

Meeting Executive Committee Welfare
2026

Muchilottu Bhagavathi Theyyam — Grand Festival, Taliparamba

The annual Muchilottu Bhagavathi theyyam was performed over three days at Taliparamba kavu with participation from 400+ community members. MDB coordinated logistics, accommodation, and welfare support.

Theyyam Festival Taliparamba
2025

Heritage Documentation Project — 108 Kaavus Survey

MDB launched a heritage documentation project surveying all 108+ Muchilottu Kaavus across Kannur. The project collected oral histories, temple records, and photographic documentation of sacred groves.

Heritage Documentation Survey
2025

Member Welfare Fund Disbursement — Kannur South

The MDB welfare committee disbursed support funds to 120 member families across Kannur South region following the 2025 welfare drive. Funds were utilized for medical assistance, education, and livelihood support.

Welfare Kannur South Community
2024

Theyyam Performer Welfare Initiative Launch

MDB introduced a dedicated welfare initiative for registered theyyam performers affiliated with Muchilottu Kaavus. The program provides annual support, insurance coverage, and health check-up camps.

Theyyam Performers Welfare Initiative 2024
2024

Digital Portal Launch — MDB Online Member Registration

MDB launched its first digital member portal enabling online registration, kaavu selection, contribution tracking, and real-time event updates. Over 500 members registered in the first month.

Digital Portal Launch Technology
2023

Inter-Kaavu Cultural Festival — Payyanur

The first inter-kaavu cultural festival brought together representatives from 60 kaavus across Kannur. The event featured theyyam showcases, traditional music, and community discussions on preservation.

Cultural Festival Payyanur Inter-Kaavu
2022

MDB Foundation — Formal Registration & Inaugural Meeting

Muchilottu Devasam Board was formally registered as a recognized community organization in Kerala. The inaugural meeting was attended by representatives from 72 founding kaavus and over 300 community members.
