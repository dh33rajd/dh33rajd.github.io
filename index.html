About MDB

Muchilottu Devasam Board is a committee platform supporting kaavu communities, pooja workers, and theyyam traditions through organized communication and welfare initiatives across the Kannur region.

Community Mission
🌿

What MDB Does

  • Coordinates communication among kaavus and members
  • Publishes festival and event updates across regions
  • Organizes member support programs and board initiatives
  • Builds a structured network for 108+ Muchilottu Kaavus
